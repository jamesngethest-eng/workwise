Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Site owner: add your business name and contact email here before launch, and have a lawyer review these pages for the laws that apply to you. This text describes what the software actually does.

What we collect

DataWhy
Name, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text)To create and secure your account and to let you recover it.
Profile details you choose to add: title, location, about, skills, portfolio linkTo show hirers who you are when you send a proposal.
Jobs you post, proposals you send, CVs and photos you attachTo run the marketplace.
Messages and any files, photos, videos or folders you send in chatTo let you talk with the other person. Only the people in that conversation can open them.
Plan payments: the reference you enter, plan and amountTo confirm payment for a paid plan.
Your IP address and security events (blocked requests, failed sign-ins, password resets)To protect the site from abuse. Kept in the site’s database for a limited time.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers. Your email address is hidden from hirers unless you switch on “Let hirers see my email” in your profile.

Who can see what

Hirers can see your name, profile details, proposal, rate and attachments for jobs you apply to. Other members cannot read your messages. The site administrator can manage jobs and payments and may need to access data to keep the service running or to deal with abuse.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you open the site. If account-recovery emails are enabled, your email address is passed to the email provider the site owner chose (for example Brevo or Gmail) only to deliver those emails.

Your choices

Open Edit profile on the site to download all your data or to delete your account. Deleting removes your profile, jobs, proposals, conversations, chat files and payment records and signs you out everywhere. Recovery codes expire after 10 minutes.

Keeping data safe

The site uses HTTPS, hashed passwords, rate limiting, sign-in lockouts, bot traps and IP blocking, and stores chat files where only conversation members can download them. No system is perfectly secure. Do not send highly sensitive information such as card numbers or government ID through chat.

Cookies

Workwise sets one cookie, workwise_session, which keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary, is not used for tracking and is removed when you sign out (or after 8 hours, or 30 days if you choose “Remember me”). Because we use no tracking or advertising cookies, there is nothing optional to switch off. Your browser may also remember a few preferences (such as saved jobs) in local storage on your own device.

Terms of Service

By using Workwise you agree to: provide accurate information; keep your password private; not post unlawful, misleading or abusive content; not upload malware or attempt to break, scan or overload the site; and not use bots or automated tools to create accounts or messages. We may block accounts or IP addresses that do. Files that could run programs (such as .exe) are not allowed in chat.

Paid plans. Workwise is free until the date shown on the Plans page. After that, accounts without an active paid plan use the Free plan, which has lower limits. Plan payments are confirmed manually by the administrator and plans last the number of months you paid for. Contact the administrator about refunds.

Work between members. Workwise helps hirers and freelancers find each other. Agreements, payments for the work itself and disputes are between the two of you.
